Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, what we collect when you use a GoRefer referral link or fill in our form, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us to erase it.

Who collects your information

Passive Income Financial Solutions Private Limited ("PIFS", "we", "us") — a Zerodha Authorised Person (NSE AP reg. no. {{ NSE_AP_NO }}) — collects and processes your information through this website (GoRefer) when you click a referral link or submit the account-opening form.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address, if you enter them in the form.
  • Your IP address and approximate city, stored as a separate, erasable record — never mixed into your click/visit history.
  • Click and page-visit events (e.g. link opened, landing page viewed, "Continue to Zerodha" tapped). These events never contain your name, phone number or email — they are kept as an anonymous activity trail.
  • A first-party cookie (visitor_id) set in your browser so we recognise the same visit — no third-party tracking cookie is used.

Why we collect it (purpose limitation)

Only to manage the referral and to help you open a Zerodha demat & trading account. We do not use your information for anything else — no unrelated marketing, no sale of your data.

Who we share it with

  • Zoho CRM — our CRM, used to track your lead and your Zerodha account-opening status. Zoho processes your data on our behalf; it does not sell it.
  • Wati (on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform) — used only to deliver WhatsApp messages about your referral or account opening, if you contact us or opt in on WhatsApp.
  • We never sell your information to anyone, and we never share it for a purpose unrelated to your referral or your Zerodha account.

How long we keep it

If you do not go on to open a Zerodha account, we automatically anonymise or erase your prospect information (name, email, city, IP address) after 12 months. Your mobile number is replaced with a non-reversible code, not left in plain form. If you do open an account through us, we keep the business record of that account as required by law and by our regulator — this is not marketing data.

Your consent

By ticking the consent box on our form, you agree to us contacting you about opening a Zerodha account and to us processing your details as described on this page. You may withdraw consent at any time using the erasure request option below — this does not affect anything already done with your consent before you withdraw it.

Ask us to erase your data

You can ask us to erase your information at any time. Message our WhatsApp Business number {{ wati_business_number_display }} with your registered mobile number, and we will erase your name, email, city and IP address (a converted Zerodha account record is a regulatory record and is handled separately, on request).
